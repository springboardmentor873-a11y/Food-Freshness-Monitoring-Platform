General Freshness Signs

🍅 Fruits: Look for firm texture, vibrant color, no visible mold or bruising.
🥦 Vegetables: Should be crisp, not wilted, with no dark or slimy patches.
👃 General rule: Trust your nose — an off smell is a reliable spoilage signal.

❄️ Store fruits and vegetables separately — some fruits release ethylene gas that speeds up spoilage.
🥶 Keep the fridge between 1–4°C for most produce.
🧊 Leafy greens last longer wrapped in a slightly damp paper towel.

FreshCheck uses a deep learning model (ResNet50) trained to classify food images as fresh or rotten, helping reduce food waste and support smarter food storage decisions.
